The Bat-Family

Batman

Bruce Wayne

Billionaire playboy by day, Gotham City's feared vigilante by night. After witnessing his parents' murder, Bruce traveled the world to master martial arts and detective skills, returning to wage a one-man war on crime.

  • Voiced by: Kevin Conroy

Robin

Dick Grayson

A former circus acrobat whose parents were murdered by mobster Tony Zucco. Bruce Wayne took him in as his ward, training him to become his crime-fighting partner. Known for his lighter attitude and agility.

  • Voiced by: Loren Lester

Batgirl

Barbara Gordon

The highly intelligent daughter of Commissioner James Gordon. She independently takes up the mantle of Batgirl, eventually earning Batman's trust and becoming a vital member of the team.

  • Voiced by: Melissa Gilbert

Rogues Gallery

Poison Ivy ♥

Dr. Pamela Isley

A radical eco-terrorist and brilliant botanist who uses plant toxins and mind-controlling pheromones for her criminal activities. She values plant life over human life and often teams up with her best friend, Harley Quinn.

  • Voiced by: Diane Pershing

Catwoman

Selina Kyle

A world-class jewel thief and animal rights activist. She walks a fine line between villain and anti-hero, maintaining a complex, flirtatious, and adversarial relationship with Batman.

  • Voiced by: Adrienne Barbeau

Two-Face

Harvey Dent

Once Gotham's noble District Attorney and one of Bruce Wayne's closest friends. After a chemical explosion scarred half his face, his suppressed dark persona took over. He now makes life-or-death decisions based on the flip of a scarred coin.

  • Voiced by: Richard Moll

Bane

Unknown

A highly intelligent assassin originating from a brutal Cuban prison. He is enhanced by the super-steroid "Venom," which grants him immense physical strength. He was hired by Rupert Thorne to break the Batman.

  • Voiced by: Henry Silva

Clayface

Matt Hagen

A tragic figure who was once a popular actor. Addicted to an experimental cosmetic chemical to hide his facial disfigurement, corrupt businessman Roland Daggett attempted to kill him with an overdose. Instead, Hagen transformed into a massive, shapeshifting mud monster.

  • Voiced by: Ron Perlman
